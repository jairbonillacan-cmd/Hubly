## Paréntesis

Dos formas que reúnen y protegen las marcas.

Archivo: hubly-06-parentesis.png

### Prompt final

Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE outstanding logo proposal for the real brand Hubly, an open-source platform that brings a marketing agency's client brands, content calendars and approvals into one workspace.
Art direction: Design a compact, original h monogram from TWO bold opposing bracket-like shapes. Each bracket has a vertical stem and one short inward horizontal arm; their offset inner arms almost meet to form the bridge of a lowercase h, with an intentional narrow negative-space gap. Outer corners softly rounded, inner joints precise. A simple, architectural, highly legible h rather than a chain, link, infinity sign, or literal code brackets. One forest green #0a7a55 symbol. Pair with an expertly drawn lowercase hubly wordmark in deep green #10291f, medium-bold humanist grotesque with slightly narrow proportions, crisp flat terminals, subtle personality and excellent kerning. Refined Swiss editorial character with warmth.
Design quality: The restraint, optical precision, and visual intelligence of an excellent independent identity studio. Prefer one memorable design idea executed impeccably. The logo must feel intentional and ownable, immediately readable at small sizes, not like a generic SaaS logo generator. Strong silhouette, consistent corner treatment, precise optical balance, and substantial clear negative space. Completely flat vector-style artwork with perfectly clean edges and uniform solid fills.
Composition: One single large horizontal logo centered on a wide landscape canvas, comfortable margins on every side, symbol to left of wordmark unless typography-only is specified. The full logo should occupy about 75% of canvas width. Present ONLY the logo against a uniform pure white #ffffff background.
Text exactly: "hubly" in lowercase, spelled h u b l y. No other text, no tagline, no proposal title, no numbers.
Avoid: mockups, branding boards, presentation grids, alternate versions, gradients, glow, shadows, paper texture, bevels, perspective, 3D, decorative flourishes, hairline details, stock chain-link symbols, infinity loops, floating decorative dots, AI sparkles, and watermarks.
